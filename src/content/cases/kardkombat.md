## Overview

### A card game you play like a fighting game

Each fighter brings a deck built around their own signature combos, so picking a fighter means picking a play style. A match is a best of three, and a round takes just a few minutes.

- **Cards are damage** — you win by emptying your hand, and every hit you take adds a card to it.
- **Answer or take the hit** — every attack has to be met with the same card or a block.
- **The clock keeps shrinking** — the time to move gets shorter as the round goes on.
